# **Hunter**

The  **Hunter** ("Hu") class is a standard melee class. Boasting a varied arsenal of strong Melee-type weaponry and a Skill Tree that promotes both offensive and defensive play, the Hunter class is well-suited for solo play.

Reaching Level 75 with this class grants the following base stat boosts to all class and characters on the same account: HP +50,  +15, and  +40

## Class Weapon Overview

Hunters use three unique weapons:  [Swords](/wiki/Swords_List "Swords List"),  [Wired Lances](/wiki/Wired_Lances_List "Wired Lances List"), and  [Partisans](/wiki/Partisans_List "Partisans List")

### Swords

A large broadsword used for heavy melee strikes. What it lacks in speed, it makes up for in raw damage. The Sword is a jack-of-all-trades weapon, possessing utility in almost any situation and being capable in a variety of areas. However, it lacks range compared to its other Hunter weapon counterparts. Swords can be powered up by the Skill **Sword Focus**, which grants them faster charge speeds and a variety of bonuses for staying on the offensive.

Sword's Weapon Action is *Guard*. By holding the Weapon Action Button, any attacks dealt to you will inflict sharply reduced damage and minimal knockback at the cost of PP.

### Wired Lances

A pair of blades hooked to handles by an extendable cable. The specialized blades are capable of striking targets far and wide, at the cost of some raw damage. Wired Lances also excel in grappling targets, manipulating their positions and using them to strike other foes around them. Wired Lances can be powered up by the Skill **Wired Lance Focus**, which allows them to expend Focus built up by attacking to boost the strength of their grapple-type attacks.

Wired Lance's Weapon Action is *Guard*. By holding the Weapon Action Button, any attacks dealt to you will inflict sharply reduced damage and minimal knockback at the cost of PP.

### Partisans

A long, bladed spear. Despite not having the sheer strength of a Sword, the Partisan makes up for it in its other attributes, with a solid attack speed and great range and mobility with its Photon Arts. The Partisan user can twirl their weapon following the usage of an attack or Photon Art to quickly restore PP, granting them excellent battlefield control as well. Partisans can be powered up by the Skill **Partisan Focus**, which allows them to expend Focus built up by twirling their Partisan to boost the strength of their Photon Arts.

Partisan's Weapon Action is *Guard*. By holding the Weapon Action Button, any attacks dealt to you will inflict sharply reduced damage and minimal knockback at the cost of PP.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

* Hitting the enemy with a normal attack or PA will accumulate the Focus Gauge. The gauge decreases overtime if no connecting hits are made.

* Cross Cut also gains extra damage depending on the Focus level as well.

#### **Wired Lance Focus**

**Focus Skill.** Consumes a Focus bar to increase the power of grappling PAs.

| Icon | Skill Name | Prerequisite | Skill level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 |
|  | Wired Lance Focus | - | - |

* Hitting the enemy with a normal attack or a non-holding PA will accumulate the Focus Gauge. Using Perfect Attacks builds Focus quicker.
* Maximum Focus gauge is 3 slots, and using a hold type PA will consume one Focus gauge slot to increase the power.
* The power increase on hold type PAs is 130%.
* List of PAs boosted: Wire Driver, Grapple Kick, Heavenly Drop, Cyclone Driver, Twist Driver, Shocktether.

#### **Partisan Focus**

**Focus Skill.** Consumes a Focus bar to increase the power and range of PAs.

| Icon | Skill Name | Prerequisite | Skill level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 |
|  | Partizan Focus | - | - |

* Gain Focus by maintaining [Guard](#guard) or by letting the animation you do after every attack play out.
* Maximum Focus gauge is 3 slots, and using a PA will consume one Focus gauge slot to increase the power and range.
* Accumulated Focus gauge does not decrease other than using PAs.
* The power increase on most PAs is 110%. Exceptions to this rule:
  + Rain of Tears: Only final strike is boosted.
  + Sacred Skewer and Zero Sacred Skewer: boost is 120%.
  + Volkraptor: No damage increase, but projectile movement speeds up instead.

* Upon Perfect Guarding, the endlag from guarding the attack disappears, and a shockwave called Guard Arts will occur, damaging enemies within the effective range.
* Perfect Guarding will make you invincible for roughly 1 second, and will not allow to guard attacks during that timeframe.
* Perfect Guarding will not prevent you from guarding unguardable attacks.
